I have always struggled with star halos and have tried lots of workflows one can find on youtube or on the internet generaly. 
Tedious the work was, with limited and unsatisfatory outcomes. The little HaloHalo tool is my attempt to squash these
nasty gremlins of stray optics I've learnt to hate. 

Now the removal is mostly a matter of a few clicks. The best results can be achieved with linear images but the tool works with stretched ones as well.


### Installation
https://apeia.eu/astro-tools/halohalo

### A Few Examples
