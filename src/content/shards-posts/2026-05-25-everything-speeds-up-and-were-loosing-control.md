A reflection on https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2n41YjR5QfU and my own two day successful effort making a portion of my work redundant.

Current AI coding tools radically increased our ability to churn out code, new functionalities and democratized the power to build things. 
At the same time our ability to understand how everything actualy work is decreasing due to sheer volume of changes and our own lazyness. 
It is certain that there will be the time when not even actual knowledge decreases, but the potential one plumets too. Only handful of "weirdos"
will embark on a turtle tower trip.

We've been there countless of times. Almost nobody cares about how transistors or trains work - until they don't. We've been creating countles
abstraction layers in order to do more and more powerful things. But now it feels a little diferent. Until now, there was a potential path 
to understanding through more or less living heritage. Not dissimilar to WW2 rememberance of why we have built the institutions we did. 
There is always a slow decay of collective memory but AI, seems like, speeds the process so much that it feels like a sudden rift.

## But what if we stopped caring?

Intellectual control was never comprehension of everything. It was always comprehension within a boundary we operated. Everything else falls
down to empirical trust. So what if we do once more what we've done dozens of times already? Just move the boundary a level up again and govern
what left bellow by behaviour rather than understanding. I always felt strong when I was able to potentially integrate abstraction layers into a
coherent internal story. Now it looks like it is time to jump and fly. And let that story to a few "weirdos". This "potential" might have been an ilussion anyway.

OK, then let go 

[Observability, invariants, bounded blast radius, fast feedback: with those, a system can be steered without being understood.](https://sreinsights.io/ai-doesnt-break-software-engineering-it-moves-the-constraints/)

There is a little value stream: **intent->specification->contracts->implementation->operation**



The biggest perceived effort is often in the implementation phase. The higher the phase in the food chain the less rigorous approach is often taken. 
While AI can be and is used at every step, the closer to the intent we are the more important the human input is and more understanding is needed.
