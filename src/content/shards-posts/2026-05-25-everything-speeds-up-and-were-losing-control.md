A reflection on https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2n41YjR5QfU and my own two-day successful effort making a portion of my work redundant.

Current AI coding tools radically increased our ability to churn out code, new functionalities and democratized the power to build things. 
At the same time our ability to understand how everything actually works is decreasing due to the sheer volume of changes and our own laziness. 
It is certain that there will be a time when not only actual knowledge decreases, but the potential one plummets too. Only a handful of "weirdos"
will embark on a turtle tower trip.

We've been there countless times. Almost nobody cares about how transistors or trains work - until they don't. We've been creating countless
abstraction layers in order to do more and more powerful things. But now it feels a little different. Until now, there was a potential path 
to understanding through more or less living heritage. Not dissimilar to WW2 remembrance of why we have built the institutions we did. 
There is always a slow decay of collective memory but AI, it seems, speeds the process up so much that it feels like a sudden rift.

## But what if we stopped caring?

Intellectual control was never comprehension of everything. It was always comprehension within a boundary we operated in. Everything else comes
down to empirical trust. So what if we do once more what we've done dozens of times already? Just move the boundary a level up again and govern
what's left below by behaviour rather than understanding. I always felt strong when I was able to potentially integrate abstraction layers into a
coherent internal story. Now it looks like it is time to jump and fly. And leave that story to a few "weirdos". This "potential" might have been an illusion anyway.

OK, then let go 

[Observability, invariants, bounded blast radius, fast feedback: with those, a system can be steered without being understood.](https://sreinsights.io/ai-doesnt-break-software-engineering-it-moves-the-constraints/)

There is a little value stream: **intent->specification->contracts->implementation->operation**



The biggest perceived effort is often in the implementation phase. The higher the phase in the food chain, the less rigorous the approach often taken. 
While AI can be and is used at every step, the closer to the intent we are, the more important the human input is and the more understanding is needed.
