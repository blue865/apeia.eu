* Our believes can be thought of as propabilities

* New evidence does not completely determine ones believe in a vacuum, it should update prior believes.

* Evidence (belief) restricts the space of all posibilities.

## General structure
* One has some hypothesis, and evidence (belief)
Then P(Hypothesis/given that evidence is true) ~ P(H|E) - conditional probalitiy

P\{rior\}(H) - prior probality that andy is an librarian (say only librarians can fit the description)
* P\{osterior\}(H|E) = (P\{rior\}(H)*P(E|H))  / (P\{rior\}(H)*P(E|H) + (P\{rior\}(!H)*P(E|!H))) = (P\{rior\}(H)*P(E|H))  / P(total E)

The better way to understand it is to draw a geometric (normalized) shape of all propabilities with propabilities of 
hyphotesis is true as a subset of that shape (propability is the area of that subset) - evidence restricts
that space.

Beyesians focus on modeling uncertainty as propability. Can include even uncertainities about the model itself or its parameters.
